YouTune settings

YouTune is an independent personal listening tuner for YouTube and YouTube Music. It processes audio locally and stores preferences in this browser profile.

Release controls

Use the YouTune toolbar popup to select one of 16 presets, adjust the 10-band equalizer, change preamp, bypass processing, reset the curve, or reconnect after a player change.

No inactive experimental controls are exposed in this release.

Privacy and support

YouTune does not require an account, upload audio, use microphone access, or use tab capture. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
